# Obtain an RF2 Release

1. Register for access and obtain a release from the [Australian National Clinical Terminology Service](https://implementer.digitalhealth.gov.au/ncts/native-snomed-ct-rf2-files), or use another release you are licensed to access.
2. The Australian SNOMED CT-AU edition includes the International core. Check your edition and licence before use or redistribution.
3. Keep the RF2 distribution as a ZIP for import; choose an archive containing the Full or Snapshot files you intend to load.

![Entity relationship diagram for native SNOMED CT RF2 files](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEiWriFzSe21DR_jLx9cvIaS0VCeIOQGxsN3l5gKZR4GUlacZpc0wDOZEY8jUqTm2FpLQvvsrED7bNPH3hzEaD1ew9s4v7dAp3x3ltIdKXVaf316hwRz8SAXqkPcSZftSR-gWAMuUqjfWccS9siZCBbmPUNgPQPgWwTuAR346KBdvWdo9wtg11U91dby71Q/s1600/native_snomed_ct_rf2_files_image_1.png)

The article also shows splitting a large archive with 7-Zip. If you receive split parts, reassemble or extract them to obtain the original RF2 ZIP before importing. Do not publish licensed data without permission.
